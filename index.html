Sightline

Point your camera at anything. It names what it sees — people, plants, animals, insects, vehicles, objects — and can caption and translate speech live.

Frames are analysed and discarded. Nothing is recorded or stored.

About

Sightline analyses frames in memory and discards them. No video, audio or face data is stored or sent anywhere except the single cropped frame needed for one lookup.
